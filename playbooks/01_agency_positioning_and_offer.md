# DTC Performance Agency Positioning & Offer Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity

- **Agency Archetype**: Specialized E-Commerce & Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Growth Partner.
- **Core Specialization**: Omnichannel Paid Acquisition (Meta Ads + Google Ads) & Conversion Attribution Engineering.
- **Mission Statement**: *"We engineer predictable 7-and-8-figure scale for Shopify DTC brands by combining full-funnel algorithmic media buying with rigorous unit economics."*

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

To ensure high client retention, profitable margins, and scalable results, we enforce strict qualification criteria:

### The "Sweet Spot" Client
- **Platform**: Shopify / Shopify Plus.
- **Current Monthly Revenue**: \$25,000 – \$150,000 / month.
- **Current Monthly Ad Spend**: \$5,000 – \$35,000 / month.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: \$55 – \$250+.
- **Gross Margins**: $\ge$ 65% – 80% (essential to maintain strong unit economics after ad spend and COGS).
- **Inventory & Supply Chain**: Minimum 60–90 days inventory buffer with the capacity to scale fulfillment by 300% without stockouts.
- **Product-Market Fit (PMF)**: Organic sales traction, $\ge$ 150+ genuine customer reviews, and a proven product offer.

### Red Flags / Non-Qualifying Clients (Disqualifiers)
- Dropshipping stores with long shipping times (> 7 business days).
- Gross margins below 50% (leaves no room for paid customer acquisition).
- Low AOV (< \$30) without bundles or subscription upsells.
- Unwillingness to produce or finance new creative assets (UGC, videos, photos) on a bi-weekly basis.
- Founders expecting overnight miracles without testing budgets.

## 3. High-Ticket "No-Brainer" Offer Architecture

Rather than selling generic "ad management hours", we sell a **90-Day Omnichannel Scale Sprint**.

### The Core Value Proposition:
> **"We guarantee to add at least \$50,000 in net new monthly revenue at your target Blended MER within 90 days, or we work for free until we hit it."**

### Offer Components Included:
1. **Full Creative Testing Engine**: Weekly UGC briefs, hook testing variations, static iteration, and thumbstop analysis.
2. **Meta Ads Overhaul**: Consolidation into Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC) and 3:2:2 Dynamic Creative Sandbox.
3. **Google Ads Scaling**: Performance Max segmentation by SKU margin, brand search defense, and high-intent capture.
4. **Attribution & CAPI Fix**: Server-Side Meta CAPI and GA4 setup to recover lost iOS signals.
5. **Bi-Weekly Growth Sprints**: Real-time Slack channel, bi-weekly strategic roadmap calls, and live Looker/Dashboard access.
