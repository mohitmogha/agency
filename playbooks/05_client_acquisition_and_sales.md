# Client Acquisition & Sales Engine Playbook

## 1. Overview: The Outbound & Inbound Funnel

A high-ticket DTC marketing agency does not win clients through generic spam or aggressive cold calling. We win by leading with **undeniable upfront value** using the **"Loom Video Audit" Funnel**.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 ACQUISITION ENGINE FUNNEL                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. Prospect Sourcing (Storeleads, BuiltWith, Ad Library)   │
```

## 2. Prospect Research & Qualification Workflow

### Step 1: Tool Stack
- **Storeleads.app** or **BuiltWith**: Filter by platform (`Shopify`), installed apps (`Klaviyo`, `Recharge`, `Gorgias` - signals serious business), estimated revenue (`$300k - $3M/year`).
- **Meta Ad Library**: Inspect how many ads they are currently running.
  - *Sweet spot*: Brands running between 10 and 45 active ads. (Too few = they don't test enough; Too many = already have huge internal team).
- **Google Search**: Search their brand name and primary product category. Are they bidding on their brand name? Are they in Google Shopping?

## 3. The 5-Minute "Loom Video Audit" Script

Record a video with your face bubble in the corner, sharing your screen across their Meta Ad Library, Shopify store speed, and Google Search:

### Timestamp Breakdown:
- **0:00 - 0:45 (Personalized Hook)**:
  *"Hey [Founder Name], love what you’re doing with [Brand Name], especially your recent push with the [Hero Product]. I was studying your Meta Ad Library and noticed something fascinating that could easily be costing you 20% to 30% on your customer acquisition costs..."*
- **0:45 - 2:00 (Leak #1: Creative Diversification)**:
  Show their active ads. Point out if they are over-reliant on 1 format (e.g. only static images or only 1 UGC video). Explain how fatigue kicks in without a 3:2:2 dynamic sandbox.
- **2:00 - 3:15 (Leak #2: Google Search & Brand Cannibalization)**:
  Open Google. Search their brand name. Show if competitors are stealing their top spot or show if their product titles in Google Shopping lack high-volume keywords.
- **3:15 - 4:15 (Leak #3: Attribution & CAPI)**:
  Inspect their pixel health using Meta Pixel Helper extension. Point out any missing server-side parameters or delayed events.
- **4:15 - 5:00 (Friction-Free Call to Action)**:
  *"No pitch here at all. If you’d like me to send over our complete 45-point audit checklist or jump on a quick 15-minute brainstorm to map out what a 90-day scale sprint looks like, just reply to this email or grab a slot below. Either way, hope this gave you a few quick wins for this week!"*

## 5. The 2-Call Closing Framework

### Call 1: The Diagnostic Call (20 Minutes)
- **Objective**: Qualify the lead, build rapport, uncover financial metrics, determine if they qualify for the 90-day sprint.
- **Key Questions**:
  1. *"What is your current monthly revenue, and where do you realistically want to be in the next 90 to 180 days?"*
  2. *"What are your blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) and average order value (AOV) right now?"*
  3. *"What is your gross margin percentage after COGS and shipping?"*
  4. *"What has been the biggest bottleneck stopping you from spending \$30k–\$50k/month profitably on Meta & Google?"*
  5. *"Do you have the inventory capacity if we double your order volume over the next 60 days?"*

### Call 2: The Strategy Roadmap & Pitch (45 Minutes)
- **Slide 1**: Their Current State vs. Desired 90-Day Destination.
- **Slide 2**: The Leak Matrix (Where money is leaking in Meta, Google, and tracking).
- **Slide 3**: The 90-Day Action Plan (Month 1: Rebuilding Foundation & CAPI; Month 2: Creative Scaling & PMax; Month 3: Profit Maximization).
- **Slide 4**: Financial Projection Model (Showing expected revenue increase vs. ad spend).
- **Slide 5**: The Offer & Risk-Reversal Guarantee (Base retainer + performance fee; if we don't hit target ROAS, we work free).
