# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT & SCOPE OF WORK (SOW)

**EFFECTIVE DATE**: __________________, 2026  
**BETWEEN**:  
**AGENCY**: [Agency Legal Name], a digital marketing firm ("Agency"), and  
**CLIENT**: [Client Legal Entity / Brand Name], a corporation/LLC ("Client").

---

## 1. Scope of Services
Agency shall provide omnichannel digital advertising and attribution management services for Client's e-commerce store, including:
1. **Meta Advertising**: Campaign architecture, 3:2:2 dynamic creative testing, Advantage+ Shopping campaign management, and dynamic catalog retargeting.
2. **Google Advertising**: Performance Max campaign optimization, product feed enrichment, high-intent non-brand search, and brand search defense.
3. **Attribution & Analytics**: Server-side Meta Conversions API (CAPI), GA4 e-commerce tracking, and blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) reporting.
4. **Creative Strategy**: Bi-weekly UGC briefs, video hook variation recommendations, and static ad concept scripts.

---

## 2. Compensation & Billing Terms

### 2.1 Monthly Base Retainer
Client shall pay Agency a recurring monthly base retainer of **\$[Retainer Amount, e.g., \$3,500.00] USD**. The first month’s retainer is due upon contract execution prior to account takeover. Subsequent months are billed on the 1st of each calendar month.

### 2.2 Performance Fee (Scale Bonus)
In addition to the base retainer, Agency shall receive a performance fee calculated as:
- **[e.g., 6%] of gross ad spend scaled above $[Ad Spend Baseline, e.g., \$25,000.00] USD** in any given billing cycle, contingent upon maintaining a minimum 30-day Blended MER of **[e.g., 3.2x]**.
- Performance fees are reconciled and invoiced on the 1st day of the following month with Net-7 payment terms.

### 2.3 Direct Ad Spend Responsibility
Client shall directly link their corporate credit card or payment method to Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads. **Agency does not advance, finance, or pay ad spend on behalf of Client.**

---

## 3. Intellectual Property & Account Ownership
1. **100% Client Ownership**: Client shall at all times retain sole and exclusive ownership of all ad accounts, pixel data, customer lists, Google Merchant Center accounts, and creative assets produced.
2. **No Hostage Policy**: Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, Agency shall immediately relinquish administrative access and shall never lock, hold, or restrict Client's access to their proprietary assets.

---

## 4. Term, Commitment & Termination
1. **Initial Growth Sprint**: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of **ninety (90) days** ("Initial Sprint").
2. **Rolling Renewal**: Following the Initial Sprint, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis.
3. **Termination**: Either party may terminate this Agreement following the Initial Sprint by providing **thirty (30) days advance written notice**.

---

## 5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
Both parties agree to protect and keep confidential all non-public information, including financial metrics, conversion rates, customer data, and proprietary marketing methodologies.
