# E-Commerce Brand Diagnostic & Audit Checklist (45 Points)

Use this rigorous 45-point checklist during prospect audits and new client onboarding. Score each section from 0–100%.

---

## Section 1: Meta Ads Account Architecture (10 Points)

- [ ] **1. Account Consolidation**: Are there $\le 3$ active campaigns running per country market, or is the account fragmented into 10+ overlapping ad sets?
- [ ] **2. Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT)**: Is the client using 3:2:2 ABO sandbox tests, or running arbitrary single ads?
- [ ] **3. Advantage+ Shopping (ASC)**: Is an ASC active with an existing customer budget cap set between 5% and 10%?
- [ ] **4. Social Proof Preservation**: Are scaling ads using the original Post ID (retaining comments/shares) or duplicating fresh ads?
- [ ] **5. Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)**: Is there an active catalog campaign retargeting ViewContent and AddToCart (14-day window)?
- [ ] **6. Creative Format Diversity**: Does creative spend break down across at least 4 formats (UGC Video, Founder Explainer, Comparison Graphic, Press Carousel)?
- [ ] **7. Thumbstop Rate**: Are winning video ads maintaining $\ge 30\%$ 3-second hook rate?
- [ ] **8. Click-Through Rate**: Is outbound link CTR $\ge 1.5\%$?
- [ ] **9. Frequency Health**: Is prospecting frequency $\le 1.8$ over a 7-day window?
- [ ] **10. Attribution Setting**: Is the attribution window configured to 7-day click + 1-day view?

---

## Section 2: Google Ads & Merchant Center (10 Points)

- [ ] **11. Performance Max Segmentation**: Are high-margin and hero SKUs segmented via custom labels, or are all products lumped into one PMax?
- [ ] **12. Audience Signals**: Does PMax utilize first-party customer lists, in-market segments, and competitor search terms?
- [ ] **13. Product Title SEO**: Do GMC titles contain Brand + Gender + Product Type + Key Features + Color/Size?
- [ ] **14. Merchant Center Health**: Are there zero product disapprovals, policy warnings, or missing GTIN/MPN errors?
- [ ] **15. Brand Search Defense**: Is there a dedicated exact-match Brand campaign preventing competitors from poaching traffic?
- [ ] **16. Non-Brand Search STAGs**: Are high-intent search campaigns focused on solution-oriented terms with exact/phrase matching?
- [ ] **17. Negative Keyword Lists**: Is there a negative keyword list containing common waste terms (`free`, `login`, `careers`, `wholesale`)?
- [ ] **18. Conversion Tracking Integrity**: Does Google Ads import transactions directly from GA4 or native Google Tag with dynamic basket values?
- [ ] **19. Enhanced Conversions**: Is Google Ads Enhanced Conversions enabled with hashed first-party customer inputs?
- [ ] **20. Bid Strategy Maturity**: Are campaigns running on Maximize Conversion Value with a realistic target ROAS?

---

## Section 3: Tracking, CAPI & Attribution (8 Points)

- [ ] **21. Meta CAPI Status**: Is Conversions API active and reporting both server and browser events?
- [ ] **22. Event Match Quality (EMQ)**: Is the Purchase EMQ score $\ge 8.0 / 10$?
- [ ] **23. Event Deduplication**: Are browser and server events passing matching `event_id` parameters with 100% deduplication rate?
- [ ] **24. GA4 E-Commerce Events**: Are `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `begin_checkout`, and `purchase` reporting with exact monetary values?
- [ ] **25. Payment Gateway Exclusions**: Are `paypal.com`, `shopify.com`, and `stripe.com` excluded as referral sources in GA4?
- [ ] **26. UTM Standardization**: Are Meta and Google ad links utilizing consistent UTM campaign/source/medium naming conventions?
- [ ] **27. Single Source of Truth**: Is the brand tracking blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) alongside platform ROAS?
- [ ] **28. First-Party Tracking Tool**: Is a platform like Triple Whale or Northbeam installed for multi-touch attribution?

---

## Section 4: Shopify Store Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) (10 Points)

- [ ] **29. Mobile Page Load Speed**: Does the homepage and PDP load in under 2.5 seconds on a 4G mobile connection?
- [ ] **30. Clear Above-the-Fold Value Proposition**: Can a first-time visitor understand what the product does within 3 seconds?
- [ ] **31. Sticky Add-to-Cart Button**: Is there a persistent sticky CTA on mobile PDPs when scrolling past the fold?
- [ ] **32. Social Proof & Reviews**: Are star ratings, customer reviews, and UGC photos visible near the primary purchase button?
- [ ] **33. Risk Reversal**: Are free shipping thresholds, money-back guarantees, and return policies clearly displayed?
- [ ] **34. Bundle & Upsell Mechanisms**: Is there an in-cart or post-purchase upsell app (e.g., CartHook, ReConvert) increasing AOV?
- [ ] **35. Checkout Friction**: Is 1-click checkout (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay) enabled and prominent?
- [ ] **36. Customer Service Access**: Is there live chat, FAQ accordions, or instant customer support accessible on the checkout path?
- [ ] **37. Cart Abandonment Sequences**: Are SMS and email flows (Klaviyo) triggered at 1 hour, 12 hours, and 24 hours?
- [ ] **38. Post-Purchase Flow**: Is there an immediate order confirmation and welcome series building brand loyalty?

---

## Section 5: Unit Economics & Supply Chain Readiness (7 Points)

- [ ] **39. Gross Margins**: Is the gross margin after COGS $\ge 65\%$?
- [ ] **40. Average Order Value (AOV)**: Is the current store AOV $\ge \$60$?
- [ ] **41. Repeat Purchase Rate**: Do $\ge 20\%$ of customers purchase again within 90 days?
- [ ] **42. Inventory Buffer**: Is there enough inventory on hand or in production for 60–90 days of 2x scaling?
- [ ] **43. Fulfillment Speed**: Are orders picked, packed, and shipped within 24–48 hours?
- [ ] **44. Creative Production Capacity**: Can the client produce 4–8 new video and static assets every month?
- [ ] **45. Founder Mindset**: Is the leadership team aligned on a 90-day testing horizon rather than day-to-day panic?
